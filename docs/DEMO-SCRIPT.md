# Demo script: A to Z in about 15 minutes

Open **https://asad-observation-hub.vercel.app**. The system is in demo mode: sample ratings under real staff names,
labelled "Demo" on every page. Nothing here is a real judgement.

## 1. Who sees what (1 minute)
- The sign-in page lists the people you can view the system as: Administrator, Ms. Ayan, Ms. Marwa, a teacher, and
  "View as another teacher".
- Say: "Each person sees only what they should. Teachers see only their own feedback. Observers see their own
  visits and the school dashboard. The administrator sees everything."

## 2. The observer during a lesson (5 minutes), as **Ms. Ayan**, ideally on a phone
0. **The welcome story** opens by itself, in four parts. On the second part, let them **tap a level** and watch the
   form's own words appear. The third part types a letter. The last part shows Share and the teacher's notice. Press
   **Open my next visit**, or Skip intro and continue below.
1. **My visits:** the week by day, with status marks. Point at **Your first observation**: four empty boxes that
   will tick themselves as you go.
2. Open **Ms. Walaa** (Thursday, Period 1, not started).
3. Fill the lesson details. Tap a level on a few criteria and show that **the form's exact descriptor** appears.
4. Show the **"Saved"** status at the top. Turn on airplane mode, tap a rating: **"Offline · kept on this device"**.
   Turn airplane mode off and it saves.
5. Rate the rest quickly, then show the **judgement table** at the bottom, worked out automatically.
6. Press **Write summary with AI**. In about 10 seconds you get strengths, improvements and a 150 to 220 word
   letter, built only from the ratings and comments. Edit a word to show it is editable.
7. **Mark complete**, then **Sign electronically**, then **Share with Ms. Walaa**. Open the **PDF**: the official
   form with every rating ticked.

## 3. The teacher (3 minutes): switch role to **Ms. Walaa** (banner, Switch role, View as another teacher)
0. The teacher's welcome story, in English with Arabic under each part: try the **عربي** switch and the **Sign**
   button.
1. The bell shows a new notice. Open the observation.
2. Read the letter, strengths and improvements. Press **عربي** to show the Arabic version.
3. **Sign electronically**, then write a one-line **reflection** and send it.
4. Switch back to Ms. Ayan and open the visit: the teacher's signature and reflection are there.
5. Open **My visits**: the first-steps boxes are now all ticked.

## 4. The school leadership view (5 minutes), as **Ms. Ayan** or **Administrator** (they now see the same)
1. **Register:** every visit, the Friday summary, logins.
2. **Dashboard:**
   - The school's strengths and weaknesses by area and criterion.
   - The heatmap of every teacher against every criterion.
   - Experts and teachers needing support.
3. **Suggested workshops:** open one, then **Schedule and notify**. It appears under **Workshops**. The completed
   October workshop shows whether attendees improved afterwards.
4. **Peer pairing:** press **Introduce** on one pair.
5. **Teachers:** open one teacher to see progress since the last observation, criterion by criterion.
6. **Setup:**
   - Show the visitation schedule import (paste a table or upload a CSV).
   - Adding people.
   - Rubric wording, with history.
7. **Audit log:** every rating change, who and when.
8. **Export:** everything to Excel at any time.

## 4b. Team leader observations (2 minutes), as **Ms. Ayan**
1. Open **Team leader observations**: forms from team leaders who observe colleagues, kept apart from the official ones.
2. Use **download a sample completed form**, then upload it. In about 15 seconds every rating is read, and the
   names are matched to staff.
3. Check it next to the original and press **Checked: confirm and save**.
4. Show **How each team leader rates**: who rates more generously or more strictly than the coordinators.
5. In **Setup**, upload a schedule as Excel, PDF or a photo: every row is read and shown for checking before import.

## 5. Going live
- When the school is ready, the administrator opens **Go live** (banner, top right), types **GO LIVE** and confirms.
- All demo data is deleted. Password sign-in opens with the real Week 10 schedule.
- To present again before going live, use **Reset the demo** on the same page.
